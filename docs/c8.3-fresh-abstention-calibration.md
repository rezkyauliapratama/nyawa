# C8.3 - Fresh Synthetic Abstention Calibration

## Why C8.3 is separate

C8.2 used the r2 runtime, where `VectorSearchAt` could lose HNSW distance ordering after a namespace filter. PR #71 fixes the ordering and adds a regression test. The C8.2 holdout is consumed and must not be used to tune or validate another policy.

C8.3 uses a new deterministic corpus, new identifiers/markers/query text, and a fresh disjoint calibration/holdout split. It runs only after PR #71 is merged and pins the rebuilt runtime by digest.

## Corpus and gates

- 90 new synthetic records and 120 unique queries; 60 answerable and 60 no-answer.
- 60 calibration and 60 holdout queries; 30 answerable and 30 no-answer in each split.
- Categories: exact identifier, semantic paraphrase, bilingual paraphrase, namespace isolation, active-vs-retired filtering, plus five no-answer subtypes.
- Calibration-only sweeps include the C8.1 scalar signals plus joint VectorRank/FTS5Rank cutoffs, optionally conjoined with score, RRFScore, or lexical coverage.
- Gates remain Recall@5 >= 0.90, exact-ID recall = 1.00, no-answer FPR <= 0.05, namespace/forbidden-marker leakage = 0.
- Choose on calibration only; evaluate the selected policy once on holdout. If none passes, report FAIL and stop. Do not tune on holdout.
